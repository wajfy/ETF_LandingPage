Conversion Landing Page

The goal is a landing page comparing NYSE-traded ETFs for a Czech retail investor. This visitor arrives from an ad on mobile and only spends a few seconds on the page. The page should work as a lead magnet: offer the visitor something of value and get their contact details in exchange. A nice design isn’t enough. The page has to be optimized for collecting contacts. It also must not look like a default Claude output. Both the design and the copy should be carefully thought through for this target audience. When evaluating, my main question will be whether we could launch ads pointing to it tomorrow.

Define the target audience, what the visitor gets in exchange for their contact, and at what point you ask for it. The value should be obvious before the visitor fills in the form. Also design two versions of the ad. The first screen of the page should follow on from the ad’s promise and immediately communicate what the visitor will get.

The choice of interactive elements is up to you, for example a fund comparison, a fee calculator or a portfolio builder. Each of them should lead to the contact form. The form should have as few fields as possible. Also think through what the visitor receives after submitting it.

Use real data and cite the source. Track the whole journey from arrival to form submission. In the README, explain the order of the sections, state the expected conversion rate, and list three A/B test hypotheses ranked by expected impact. Deploy the page on free hosting, for example Vercel.
